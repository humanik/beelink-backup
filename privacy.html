Privacy policy

beelink-backup is a personal tool used only by its owner.

  • It accesses Google Drive with the drive.file scope, limited to files the app itself creates.
  • Backup data is encrypted on the owner's server before upload and is stored only in the owner's own Google Drive.
  • No data is collected, shared with, or sold to any third party. No analytics or tracking are used.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
